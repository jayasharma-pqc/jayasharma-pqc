Research

I'm a PhD student in Cybersecurity Engineering at the DTU Cryptography Group, Technical University of Denmark, supervised by Christian Majenz. My research sits at the intersection of theoretical cryptography and quantum computing. My PhD is in studying the security of cryptographic constructions in the quantum random oracle model (QROM) and post quantum constructions.

More broadly, I'm interested in public-key cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, lattice-based cryptography, and quantum information.

I completed a Master's in Mathematics at IIT Roorkee and a Bachelor's (Honours) in Mathematics at Lady Shri Ram College, University of Delhi.
